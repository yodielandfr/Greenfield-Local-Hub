About Greenfield Local Hub

Greenfield Local Hub connects local farmers and producers directly with customers in their community.

We believe in transparent pricing. Every product shows you exactly why it costs what it costs, from fair wages to seasonal supply chains

Why shop with GLH?

  • Support local farmers and reduce food miles
  • Fully transparent pricing on all products
  • Fresh, seasonal produce delivered to your door
  • Traceable products. You get to know where your food comes from, always!
